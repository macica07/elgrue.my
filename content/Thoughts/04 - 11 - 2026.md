# Getting the idea

Whew! What a messy week! I started this project a few days ago, after I was lost in my own thoughts during an exam, thinking about how cool it'd be to have a website of my very own!

Oh, I *really* was oblivious of how much it'd take to make this! Well, to be honest, it wasn't *that* much of a torture! Just... very exahusting!

## Setting up GitHub and obsidian

Oh, the calm before the storm... I was really motivated to start this! I even had the "backup" of [[My Sister]]! Although, I've got to admit. It was really irresponsible of me to start this project ***just*** during the exams week, *and* just weeks before my admission exam! What was I thinking about!? Anyways. Yesterday, I managed to get the first things up relatively quickly. And today was the hard part...

## I-I-I- Infoboxes!

Apparently, I have an addiction with infoboxes... At first, I thought "How hard could it be to add an infobox to this? Many sites already have it!". So I started looking around, and found the [Omni Infobox](https://community.obsidian.md/plugins/omni-infobox) plugin. Hooray! I said, not knowing what I was getting into!

![[Konachan.com - 22030 azumanga_daioh mihama_chiyo 1.jpg|500]]
*Visual representation of me after looking at everything I had to do just to implement infoboxes*

To put it simple, [Quartz](https://github.com/jackyzha0/quartz) (The site generator this website is using, because doing websites on my own would've taken years...) doesn't really support [Obsidian](https://obsidian.md) plugins! 
And I dug, deep, deep into the most obscure sides of Reddit! (Not really..) And the only answer I got was "Just do it yourself!" (Basically). So I **had** to do a plugin, all by myself, because the only one I found was for Quartz v4! And I am using Quartz v5! 

## Conclussion

Guys, infoboxes are the worst! Just kidding, but it really was a pain in the *butt* to do all of this just to create a simple *infobox!*

But letting the mild suffering aside, I think that we shouldn't see challenges in life just as unnecessary obstacles that should be avoided. I think we should see them as a test of our skills, and an opportunity to create a better version of ourselves!

After doing all of this infobox stuff, I really feel proud not only of just what I achieved (But honestly, it's a real relief to have managed to get it to work), but also of what I learned in order to achieve it! 

I think it's good to say to not skip every challenge life puts in your way. As they say:

> [!Quote] Quote
> An explorer on an easy path is not an explorer at all.

...Or something like that. You guys get it!
